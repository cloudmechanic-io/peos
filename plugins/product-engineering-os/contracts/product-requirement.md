# Product requirement contract v1

The normalized product artifact is `peos/changes/<change_id>/product-requirement.md`.

It contains:

1. lifecycle frontmatter from `lifecycle.md`;
2. problem and evidence;
3. users and jobs/outcomes;
4. product current state and target state;
5. scope, non-goals, constraints, and assumptions;
6. user journeys or scenarios;
7. measurable success and guardrails;
8. acceptance criteria in observable language;
9. risks, dependencies, and unresolved questions;
10. discovery decisions and rejected alternatives.

For a quick change, keep the document concise: current behavior, target behavior, acceptance criteria, constraints, and non-goals are sufficient. For strategic work, include the supporting brief or PR/FAQ path under `source_artifacts`.
