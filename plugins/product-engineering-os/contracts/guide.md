# Guide contract v1

Guide returns a concise lifecycle decision containing:

- interpreted intent and affected outcome;
- proposed `change_id`;
- quick, feature, or strategic classification with reasons;
- current artifact states when work already exists;
- risk signals and required specialist lenses;
- the smallest next capability and any blocking human decision. Production incidents and data questions normally route to `diagnose`, not `verify`.

Guide does not create product or technical decisions itself. It may hand off immediately to the selected capability when the user’s request already authorizes that work.
